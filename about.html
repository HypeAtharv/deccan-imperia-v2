02 · Engineering Standards

Why We Build the Way We Build.

A home is the largest thing most families ever buy, so we over-engineer where it matters: an RCC earthquake-resistant frame, AAC-block masonry, granite and designer finishes, teakwood doors, UPVC windows, and concealed branded plumbing and wiring. Not to look good on a brochure — to still feel solid twenty monsoons from now.

01

Solid Foundation & Plinth

Earthquake-resistant RCC framed structure with AAC block masonry for superior thermal cooling. External double sand-faced cement plaster with waterproof seal protects against heavy monsoon winds.

02

Tactile Luxury Finishes

Branded 800 × 1600 mm vitrified tiles, granite door frames throughout, designer anti-skid bathroom tiling, and an authentic teakwood main door with bespoke nameplate and Europa-grade security locks.

03

Concealed Branded Utilities

Heavy-gauge CPVC/UPVC concealed plumbing with branded diverters in all bathrooms. Fire-resistant concealed copper wiring, modular switches, inverter provisions, and 3-track sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
