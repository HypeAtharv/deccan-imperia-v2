02 · Build Standards

Why We Over-Engineer Every Detail.

A home is the largest investment most families ever make. We refuse to cut corners behind drywall or hide subpar work under coats of paint. Every millimeter of Deccan Imperia is engineered to remain rock-solid twenty monsoons from now.

Authentic Teakwood Joinery & Brass Hardware Bespoke Millwork
Solid Granite Plinth & Approach Steps On-Site Reality
Standard 01

Earthquake-Resistant RCC & AAC Masonry

Seismic-compliant RCC framed structure built with premium-grade certified cement. High-density AAC block masonry provides 3–5°C natural thermal cooling against Pandharpur's summer heat, protected by external double sand-faced waterproof plaster.

Standard 02

Solid Teakwood & Full Granite Frames

Grand teakwood main entrance doors with bespoke brass hardware and Europa multi-point security locking. Full solid granite door and window frames throughout ensure zero termite risk and lifetime protection against moisture.

Standard 03

Large-Format Tiles & Tactile Finishes

Luxurious 800 × 1600 mm glazed vitrified tiles across living, dining, and master suites. Anti-skid designer ceramic tiling in all bathrooms, private sit-outs, and terrace decks with concealed brass floor drains.

Standard 04

Concealed Utilities & 3-Track Windows

Heavy-gauge CPVC/UPVC concealed piping with branded hot/cold diverters in all bathrooms. Fire-retardant concealed copper wiring with modular switches, inverter provisions, and sound-dampening 3-track sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
