04 · Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is there an actual sample bungalow ready to visit?

Yes. A complete sample bungalow is constructed on-site at Deccan Imperia. You can walk through the entrance porch, examine the ground floor living, kitchen, bedrooms, staircase, and step out onto the first-floor private terrace to inspect the exact materials and workmanship before booking.

Can out-of-town or NRI buyers purchase remotely?

Absolutely. A significant number of our homeowners reside in Pune, Mumbai, Solapur, and overseas. We conduct comprehensive one-on-one live video walkthroughs, provide digitized land records and sanctions, and assist with Power of Attorney (POA) and bank loans remotely.

What are the exact plot and built-up dimensions?

Each Phase 1 bungalow occupies an independent 30 × 50 ft plot (1,500 sq.ft total land area) with approximately 1,200 sq.ft of built-up area across Ground and First floors, plus an open private terrace deck. You hold individual, unencumbered freehold land ownership.

Are bank loans available for Deccan Imperia?

Yes. The project has clear municipal sanctions, non-agricultural (NA) title verification, and is eligible for home loan financing with leading nationalized and private banks (including SBI, HDFC, and others). Our sales desk assists with documentation.
