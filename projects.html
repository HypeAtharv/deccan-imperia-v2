Deccan Imperia — Luxury Row Bungalows in Pandharpur.

Deccan Imperia is a gated row of independent G+1 bungalows on the Pune–Pandharpur road, five minutes from the Vitthal temple. Each home sits on a 30 × 50 ft plot with a private terrace — the space and privacy of a bungalow, built to a specification that lasts generations. 06 · Value Proposition

The Investment Case.

Pandharpur is one of Maharashtra's busiest pilgrimage towns — lakhs of devotees visit the Vitthal-Rukmini temple every year, and that footfall drives steady, festival-led demand for quality homes and stays.

01 · Sacred Second Home

A peaceful, private haven for family during Ashadhi, Kartiki, and year-round Ekadashi pilgrimages. Just 5 minutes from the sanctum with your own parking and private terrace.

02 · Rental & Hospitality Asset

With over 32,000 monthly seekers and pilgrim rushes exceeding 10+ lakhs during major wari seasons, high-end family bungalow accommodations command premium rental yields.

03 · Land-Plus-Home Ownership

You own the 1,500 sq.ft plot and the structure outright at Phase 1 early pricing in a town undergoing major infrastructure expansion. No shared apartment depreciations.
